School
Under the hot rays of the sun. 
And sweat filled palms. 
A heavy bag to carry. 
To and fro.

Crowded stairs I climb. 
Laughter and madness. 
Cheers and crys. 
One victory story and one that pains. 

Thousands of lives all interconnected,
Don't know the person in the other room.
Don't know thier story.

A part of each's life I claim. 
A single second or a million.

A thousand hearts beating together.
A thousand stories untold to another.
Yet we love, yet we cry.
For one and another. 

Such meetings that you deem oh so common.
Are precious when you look harder.

From sitting in a seat used by another.
To becoming that another for who comes after.

One becomes a lawyer,
And another a doctor. 

Different paths that collide.
Tho they may stray.
Once same, they remain.

We walk, United.
Stay, United.

Memories, so unforgettable,
They remain, forever and after. 

From Tiny hands that yearn for the sky.
To throwing hats up high. 

We grew up.
We all did.
And so will they, one day.
