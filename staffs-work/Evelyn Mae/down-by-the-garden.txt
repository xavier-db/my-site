Down By The Garden
DOWN BY THE GARDEN is a work-in-progress [120,000+] high political fantasy perfect for fans of the deep worldbuilding in A DAY OF FALLEN NIGHT, the atmosphere of ONE DARK WINDOW, and the complex characters and discussions in THE POPPY WAR.

—

Dawn was a quiet beast, gray and monstrous. It beckoned blood with hands of smoke, its breath ashen and smelling of the dead, its voice the whisper of rain on linen.

Asi'Landra tied up the straps of Elvaly's armor with slow hands. The tent was dark, the warmth of the lamps unable to penetrate the shadows. Asi'Landra shivered, her skin pebbling, as the chill brought on by the heavy clouds above climbed up her spine.

"I will be fine, dove," Elvaly whispered. Her Skolan accent wrapped her words thickly, a sign of her own worry. She wrapped a thin gray ribbon around her wrist and tucked it underneath her armguard. For good luck. The unspoken phrase strung taut between the both of them, hanging heavy with fear.

Finished with the armor, Asi'Landra turned her companion to face her. The suns on Elvaly's freckled cheeks had been painted with a shaky hand, and the rays were longer than was usual, touching her hairline and chin. For protection.

"I only wish they would allow us to go together," Asi'Landra replied, voice soft. "Not for fear of your safety, or from doubts of your strength, simply that I wish you need not be alone."

The shorter woman nodded, her eyes searching Asi'Landra's face. "I will not be alone."

"But your company will be small."

"And secure," Elvaly assured her companion as much as herself, flicking her braids over her shoulders. Her flaming hair had been pulled tightly away from her face, the three weaves simple and thick, starting at the top of her head and trailing to her waist. "Tonight, the Linnevine queen will be dead, and we will celebrate."
