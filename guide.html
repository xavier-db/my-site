Guide & Rules

Everything you need to know before submitting your work.

About Elysian

Elysian is a creative platform that produces magazine issues with the hope of bringing light to people's opinions, thoughts, ideas, and feelings from all around the world. Overall, to challenge the way we speak, think, and view everything around us. Essentially, this platform is a place to raise your voice and be heard.

A new issue goes up every two months — the first month is for submitting, the second is for us to build that issue. For regular submissions, join our Discord (linked on the Contact page). For one-time submissions, message us on Instagram or email us via the Contact page.

By Submitting, You Are

  • Allowing and requesting editing and use of your work by staff members.
  • Retaining copyright, while allowing free use of the work by Elysian.

What Gets Your Work Rejected (or Sent Back)

  • You use artificial intelligence to generate ideas or write/create your work for you.
  • Plagiarism of any kind is discovered in your work.
  • Your work includes harmful prejudices or attacks towards other individuals.
  • You don't include proper warnings for potentially triggering topics (e.g. sexual assault, suicide, child abuse).
  • Your photographs depict direct self-harm or intense gore/violence.

What Happens After You Submit

  1. Our submissions manager reviews every piece for the issue. She's your point of contact and will email you whether your work makes the magazine or gets deferred to the next issue.
  2. Our editors go through it, checking for grammar and mistakes.
  3. If any changes are needed, we'll contact you for your permission, along with all the details for that issue.
  4. Our design team puts all the pieces together, and the issue is published on our website.

What to Include When You Submit

  • Your email (required).
  • Your name as the author — this will be displayed alongside your work.
  • Your age.
  • Your work, either as a file or written directly.
  • Any trigger warnings we should note.
  • Whether you give us permission to make edits if needed — we'll always message you back to confirm any edits before they're final.

If your work makes the issue, we'll email you back with all the details.
