About Elysian: To Be Seen

Elysian is a creative platform that produces magazine issues with the hope of bringing light to people's opinions, thoughts, ideas, and feelings from all around the world — to challenge the way we speak, think, and view everything around us. Essentially, this platform is a place to raise your voice and be heard.

For regular submissions, join the Discord via the Contact page. For one-time submissions, message us on Instagram or email us.
